Delete your OpenLens AI account & data

App: OpenLens AI (package ai.openlens.app)  ·  Developer: Niven Labs

This page explains how to request deletion of your OpenLens AI account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Send an email to contact@openlensai.org from the email address linked to your OpenLens AI account, with the subject "Account deletion request".

We will verify your request and permanently delete your account within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your account and authentication record (email address)
  • Your user profile and preferences (followed topics, language, newsfeed settings)
  • Analytics events associated with your account

Data that may be retained

Anonymized or aggregated data that can no longer be linked to you, and cached article summaries that contain no personal data, may be retained. Where retention is required by law, data is kept only for the period legally mandated.
