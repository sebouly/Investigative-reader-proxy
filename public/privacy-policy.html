Privacy Policy for OpenLens AI

Last updated: 3 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OpenLens AI ("we", "us", the "App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the OpenLens AI Android application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials, managed securely through our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Profile & preferences: Topics you follow, newsfeed settings, language preferences, and similar choices you make in the App.
  • Usage & analytics data: Anonymous or pseudonymous events about how features are used (for example, which screens are opened) to help us improve the App.
  • Content you request: Article URLs and text you ask the App to summarize, translate, or convert to audio. This content is processed to deliver the requested result.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core features: summaries, translations, audio narration, personalized newsfeeds, and digests.
  • To sync your profile and preferences across sessions.
  • To send newsfeed and transactional emails you have requested.
  • To monitor usage limits and operate the service reliably and cost-effectively.
  • To improve the App through aggregate analytics.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to operate the App. These may process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend functions.
  • Vercel — a secure proxy that processes summary, translation, and digest requests.
  • AI model providers (e.g. via OpenRouter / OpenAI) — to generate summaries, translations, and audio.
  • Email provider — to deliver transactional and newsfeed emails.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and profile information for as long as your account is active. Cached summaries may be stored to improve performance and reduce cost. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Contact below).

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. AI provider secrets are never stored on your device; sensitive operations occur on our secured backend.

6. Children's Privacy

OpenLens AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU/EEA under GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
